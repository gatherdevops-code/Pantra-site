Common Questions

Getting Started

How do I scan a receipt?
Simply open the Pantra app, tap the camera icon, and take a clear photo of your grocery receipt. Our AI will automatically extract items and add them to your pantry.

Can I share my pantry with family members?
Yes! Pantra supports household sharing. Go to Settings → Household to invite family members via email. Everyone will stay in sync about what's in your pantry.

Account & Privacy

How is my data protected?
We take privacy seriously. Receipt images are securely stored and only used to extract food items. Read our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Can I delete my account?
Yes, you can delete your account at any time from the app settings. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Features & Functionality

What if an item wasn't scanned correctly?
You can manually edit any item in your pantry. Tap on the item to update its name, quantity, or expiration date.

How do expiration reminders work?
Pantra sends smart notifications based on typical shelf life and your household's consumption patterns. You can customize reminder timing in Settings → Notifications.

Does Pantra work offline?
You can view your pantry inventory offline, but scanning receipts and syncing with household members requires an internet connection.
